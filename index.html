Disclaimer

Educational tool only — everything runs in your browser, nothing is stored, and it must never be used to access accounts or systems you don't own or have permission to test.

PassLab shows how password combinations work and why length and variety matter for real security. It runs entirely in your browser — nothing you select or generate is sent to, logged, or stored on any server. This is for learning and curiosity only, never for guessing, cracking, or accessing accounts, devices, or systems you don't own or aren't authorised to test — doing so is illegal in most countries. Combinatorics alone never protects a password: real systems should store passwords as salted, hashed values and add rate-limiting and multi-factor authentication. By using PassLab, you agree to use it responsibly and only for lawful, ethical purposes.
